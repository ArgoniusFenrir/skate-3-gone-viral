## 2. Community Guides

The Gone Viral trophy relies on a viral mechanic i.e. a player unlocks the trophy by playing online with someone who already has it.

### Source A: [PSNProfiles](https://psnprofiles.com/guide/2294-skate-3-trophy-guide#23-gone-viral)

> To earn this trophy, you'll have to play online with someone who already has obtained the trophy. Essentially, this is a "viral" trophy and is passed along like a virus.

### Source B: [PlayStationTrophies.org](https://www.playstationtrophies.org/game/skate-3/trophy/16530-gone-viral.html)

> This is a Viral trophy, meaning you will get it when you play online with somebody who already has it. Just play online until you get it, or use the boosting thread if you aren't having good luck.

### Source C: [TrueAchievements](https://www.trueachievements.com/a60781/gone-viral-achievement)

> This achievement is unlocked by skating in an online game with another player who has already been exposed to the Skate Flu.

## 4. Summary

The Gone Viral trophy operates on a viral mechanic.
It spreads from player to player purely through online contact.
Any time you share an online session with someone who already holds the trophy, the game detects that and awards it to you automatically.
Community guides confirm this behaviour, and video proof shows it unlocking within seconds of loading into a session.

**Personal experience:** Based on playing Skate 3 back in the day, (if memory serves me well) the trophy unlocks immediately after joining a FreeSkate session with an infected player.
